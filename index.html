Tips
  • Play one clear note at a time. To repeat the same note, stop briefly between them (a small gap or a new bow stroke).
  • Tap any note on the staff to jump to it.
  • Record counts you in for four beats, then moves through the piece at the tempo you set. Each note has to be the right pitch and held for its full length. Afterwards, Listen back plays your recording.
  • If quiet notes are missed, raise the mic sensitivity. If background noise turns notes red, lower it.
  • Headphones help with Hear note, so the mic doesn't pick up the speaker.
